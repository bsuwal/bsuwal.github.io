I ran the Providence Marathon last weekend. It took me 4 hrs and 24 mins. I am happy with my time, it is a PR by 24 mins.

I felt really good until mile 14, which is when I started hitting a wall. My thighs started feeling very tight, and that is when my pace started dipping too. The nice thing was that this time I did not hit a mental wall - which is when you doubt if you have it in you to even finish the race. That wall is much worse.

I also tried running with ear plugs on this time. So it was 4+ hours of just muffled sounds, leaving me alone with my demons. At around the 15 mile mark I took them off for a few minutes, but I immediately felt very over stimulated and I put them back on. Many people like listening to music on their runs - I like that too, but my airpods keep falling off. For now I like my ear plugs.

I try not to care too much about speed for my distance running, but I have been wanting to run faster for a little while now and I realized that I have never timed myself for a mile. I think this summer I will take a break from distance running and see how fast I can run shorter distances (1-3 miles).
