This is the first of hopefully many blog posts. I don't know what exactly I would blog about, but I do know that there will not be one major theme. My biggest inspiration is Cosma Shalizi's <a href="bactra.org">bactra.org</a>, where he blogs about his many diverse interests.

This blog comes from a general frustration I have had with myself about not writing more. I learn best when I write, and I retain ideas for longer, and re-learn faster when I read what I have myself written. And beyond my academic wants there are just so many things I want to read and write about, and a blog will I hope help me organize my thoughts properly in a nice, organized manner. And if it helps anyone else in the process, even better.

Here we go!
