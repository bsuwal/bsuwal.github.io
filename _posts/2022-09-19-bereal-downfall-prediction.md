People are super excited about BeReal these days. Their glowing reviews are basically exactly what BeReal advertises itself to be. That it is a great way to check in with close friends. They like the non-performative aspect of it. That the feed doesn't get saved so you don't spend time on it.

Too good to be true in my opinion!!

It is not BeReal that I dislike, it is its marketing (both from the app itself and its users) that it is different from any of the other parasitic apps out there that just doesn't sit well with me! The want within humans to appear witty and cool is so deep that I don't think any social media app can truly overturn its effects by the very virtue of being in the social media business. I would love to be proven wrong, but for now I remain unconvinced that BeReal is upending the status-quo.

My takes:
- Is it really non-performative though? I am sure some people jump at the notification and click the most interesting thing around them. How many people are doing homework, but then take a picture of the cat next to them instead? I bet a lot of people are clicking the most important thing around them at the moment, which is a step closer from highlight of your life/day, but only as far as highlight of the moment.
- The very fact that BeReal will interrupt your day to prompt your post (at least for those who have their phones on vibration or gasp! sound) I think is stopping you from being real in the moment. Tell me you aren't getting your Pavlovian dopamine hit when you are get the TIME TO BE REAL notification. Interesting that you have to be accessible all the time to BeReal!!
- My housemate said that since you don't have limitless scrolling you don't spend too much time on it. "But I still check it out of habit", said Housemate, perhaps forgetting that even if BeReal is not encouraging them to spend time on it the ecosystem of the attention economy has them returning to it multiple times a day! Maybe BeReal should have the posts disappear after seeing them just once (said Bhushan, perhaps forgetting that he just described 2017 Snapchat.)

To have the take that BeReal will die is scarcely a hot one. Every social media app dies, even if they claim to be an "anti-social media" app.

The attraction of BeReal, it seems to me, is to get little glimpses of what your dear friends are doing. I think that at some point people will keep adding more and more people to their BeReal, and then instead of getting a glimpse into the lives of your 10 friends who you talk to at least semi-frequently anyway you are getting mind numbingly boring peeks into the life of someone who you still care about, but don't want to see their computer screen for 5 days in a row. Then you'll be thinking, what the heck, I actually don't want this amount of detail into their life, and would rather only learn of their highlights, bringing us full circle to Instagram. RIP.

P.S. One friend said they liked how you could see what you were up to this past week/month/summer - like a dairy of random moments. This does seem cool and perhaps the only thing I like about it.

P.P.S. I would love to be proven wrong just so I get karma for enjoying writing this post with so much pompous vitriol. The rat inside my head enjoyed quickly writing this out just a bit too much.
