This is the first of hopefully many blog posts. There won't be one big unifying theme, I intend for it to be more of amalgamation of the curiosities of the moment for me.

There is a very real motivation to starting this blog. I learn best when I write, and I retain ideas for longer, and re-learn faster when I read what I have myself written. And beyond my academic wants there are just so many things I want to read and write about, and a blog will I hope help me organize my thoughts properly in an organized manner.

Here we go!
