I have intended to start a blog for quite some while. I think clearest when I write (a lesson I seem to forget all too often) and I wish to write more this year as I grow as a researcher, and to also have a repository where I can easily access my thoughts.

There may or may not be a rhyme or rhythm to these posts, and there certainly will not be a general theme that ties them together other than my idiosyncratic interests. The posts may be in Nepali, they may be in English, they may be in gibberish. They may be completely unhinged. I make no promises.

My current interests are, in no particular order:
* No Free Lunch Theorems.
* Pseudorandomness
* Spanning trees
* The sociology, politics and history of science
* The use of technology, especially smart phones. The idea of the medium as the message.

Things I wish to explore, leaving this here as a reminder:
* Entropy, and pseudo-entropy
* The Disparate Impact/ Disparate Treatment landscape
* The basics of spectral graph theory
* Expander graphs
* Average Case Complexity
* Goldreich Levin Theorem
* Hypothesis testing
* Power Laws
* Pseudorandom Functions
* Nisan-Wigderson PRG
* Birthday attacks
* Model Multiplicity

Another thing that I wish to do, is to slowly re-read a lot of the things I already have on my bookshelf, and review them. The four major themes that I want to do this are books on fairness, probability, Nepal, and the attention economy.

Other things I wish to do here:
- Make a page for the books I have read, and book reviews.
- A microblog, or what I would tweet if I had a Twitter.
