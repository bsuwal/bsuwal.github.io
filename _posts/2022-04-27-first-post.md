I once got into phase of drawing comic strips in college, which is when I made this gem. Well, it is <i>2022</i> and about damn time, so here we go.

To be fair I did have blogs both in 2014 (on blogspot!) and 2018, but each passing year made me recoil at the juvenilia so here we are with a new one, hopefully one that will keep.

I will be posting about whatever I happen to be reading or thinking at the moment: TCS, running, probability, graduate school, the weather etc. There might be some truly unhinged shit. I make no promises.

I did wonder, dripping with imposter syndrome, whether a blog was a good idea. Who would want to read it? Do I have anything interesting to say? What if I am wrong? How do other people deal with this? And then I remember that other people tweet, which made me feel better about myself.
