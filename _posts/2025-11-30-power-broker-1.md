Lizzie and I went down to New York this month on a tour of some places mentioned
in The Power Broker.


1. Uncovered railroad tracks on Riverside Drive Parkway near 125th St
The chapter _Changing_ was one of my most favorite ones in the book, where Caro
masterfully  describes the incredible ingenuity Robert Moses displays in procuring
funding for the construction of the Henry Hudson Highway. In this chapter Robert
Moses is staring down a $109 million pit of funding he will need to procure for
his long standing dream

2. The Happy Warrior Playground and the Park West Village Apartments
The blocks between 97th and 100th street bordering Amsterdam Ave and the Central
Park West. Today, among others, they house the Happy Warrior Playground, named
after Moses's mentor Al Smith, and who Caro writes as a incredibly successful
Governor of New York in the 1920s, and the Park West Village Apartments, the
public housing Moses built in the 1950s. This was a predominantly Black and Puerto
Rican neighborhood the Moses cleared, six square blocks of them.
